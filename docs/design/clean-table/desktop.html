A table, with less around it.

One place for turn state. Small player summaries. Original cards provide the color; the interface stays quiet.

  1. See both fields. Hands stay at the outer edges. Played yokai face across a clear center line, with decks, graveyards and traps beside their owner’s row.
  2. Act where you play. Tap a yokai, then choose Evoke, Release or Attack in the bottom strip. Tap a highlighted card to target it.
  3. Read on demand. Details opens the enlarged card and plain rules. Normal card selection keeps the table available.
  4. Keep testing practical. The opposing hand expands from its count. It has complete card images, with no cropped art strips.

Try the preview-state control, card taps, Attack, Details, hand toggle and allocation buttons. These demonstrate presentation only; the rules engine is unchanged.
