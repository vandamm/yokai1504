Interaction proposal

PlayDrag a hand card onto your half of the field. A spell can instead go to your trap position. Destinations appear only while selecting or dragging.

AttackDrag your yokai onto an enemy, or select it then click/tap the target. If the opposing field is empty, their health remains a direct-attack target.

SoulsA small + soul appears beside the eligible selected yokai. For a soul ability, select its filled soul and then the legal effect target; no global action menu.

InspectHold a card, or Shift-hover on desktop. Inspecting never plays it. A simple tap selects; movement starts a drag.

TrapOnly when needed: Activate / Keep hidden beside the trap. Ougi asks you to tap the donor and recipient yokai on the existing field. No screen takeover.

ResultAnimate the affected objects and values, then leave the result in history. A small unread cue in the menu opens recent events. No success toast or return dashboard.

Health is mirrored; action pips sit horizontally to its right. End turn and Undo stay at the right end of the divider. Tap/keyboard selection provides the same destinations as dragging. Unneeded controls disappear; required decisions do not.

Comments save locally. Gameplay remains intentionally static.
