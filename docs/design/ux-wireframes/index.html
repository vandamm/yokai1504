The interaction contract changes

QuestionA · Direct tableB · Guided turnC · Activity desk
Where do I begin?A card on the table or in my handAn intention: play, attack or evokeWhat changed and what needs my response
Where is my hand?Always at the bottom; expand to inspectInside the Play taskIn the move composer or Table tab
How do I commit?Choose/drop onto a legal destinationReview the completed action, then confirmSubmit a single-action draft
Where does a trap interrupt?Beside the actual trap on the boardA dedicated response workspaceAn unresolved decision in the journal
How do I undo?One nearby Undo; Cancel only clears selectionBack edits a choice; Undo reverses a committed actionEdit/discard drafts; Undo the latest committed receipt
What is the tradeoff?Fast, but contextual actions need discoveryClearer decisions, but more stepsGood for returning later, but less immediate tabletop feel

Shared baseline

Explicit actor and action count. No automatic End turn. Required decisions never disappear. Motion explains a change; a readable result remains. Drag can remain a shortcut alongside tap and keyboard selection. Hidden information never appears in a predicted result.

These are proposals, not an approved change to the game. Single-action drafts can be interrupted by traps; a whole turn is never queued.
